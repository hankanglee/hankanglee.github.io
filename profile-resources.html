Short Biography

I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from The Pennsylvania State University in 2025, advised by Hui Yang. My doctoral research spanned digital twins, multi-agent decision-making, adaptive manufacturing systems, and privacy-preserving learning. During this period, I also led the development of algorithms and learning methods for an MxD project on privacy-preserving analytics for smart manufacturing. I previously received my B.S. in Industrial Engineering and M.S. in Industrial Systems Engineering from Yonsei University, where my graduate research focused on data-driven modeling for semiconductor manufacturing.

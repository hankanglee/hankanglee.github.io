Hankang Lee

Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering

My research centers on decision processes as integral components of complex operational systems. I examine how operational conditions, interactions, uncertainty, and feedback shape decision behavior and its local and system-level consequences. Scientific understanding guides the design and evaluation of these processes, which in turn test, refine, and extend that understanding.
